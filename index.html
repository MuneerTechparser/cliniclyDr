1.Introduction

Clinicly ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Clinicly Doctor mobile application (the "App"), which connects licensed medical practitioners with patients for appointment booking, video consultations, and prescription management. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using Clinicly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2.Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — full name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Profile information — profile photo, age, gender, years of experience, speciality, and bio, added when you set up your professional profile.

2.2 Identity verification (KYC) information

To confirm your credentials as a licensed practitioner, we collect the documents you upload during KYC, including your medical degree, license to practice, and a government-issued ID. These documents are reviewed for approval before you can access the App.

2.3 Patient information

While using the App to provide care, you will access certain information about your patients, including their name, contact details, gender, age, date of birth, medical reports/files, and appointment history. This information is made available to you solely to enable you to conduct consultations and issue prescriptions.

2.4 Appointment and scheduling information

  • Your availability, weekly schedule, and blocked-off dates.
  • Appointment requests, acceptances, rejections, and rejection reasons.
  • Appointment history, including completed, cancelled, and missed appointments.

2.5 Video consultation information

We collect metadata about your video calls, such as start/end time, duration, and call status (completed, missed, or cancelled). We do not record or store the audio/video content of your consultations.

2.6 Camera and photo library access

If you choose to add or update your profile picture, the app requests access to your device's camera or photo library solely for that purpose. No images are accessed or uploaded without your explicit action.

2.7 Automatically collected information

Device identifiers, app version, push notification tokens, and basic diagnostic/crash data used to maintain app stability and performance.

3.How we use your information

We use the collected information to:

  • Verify your identity and medical credentials during KYC review.
  • Create and manage your Doctor account and professional profile.
  • Enable appointment scheduling, and the acceptance/rejection of requests.
  • Facilitate video consultations between you and your patients.
  • Enable the creation and delivery of digital prescriptions.
  • Display patient information relevant to the care you are providing.
  • Send notifications for new appointment requests, KYC status updates, reminders, and cancellations.
  • Maintain and improve the security, performance, and reliability of the App.

4.Payments

Clinicly does not process, store, or handle any consultation fees, payments, or in-app purchases between you and your patients. Clinicly does not collect, store, or have access to any payment card, banking, or financial account information.

5.Data sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information or patient data. We may share limited information as follows:

With patientsYour name, photo, speciality, experience, and bio are shown to patients to help them choose and book an appointment with you.
Verification partnersKYC documents may be shared with third-party verification providers solely to confirm your credentials.
Service providersCloud hosting, storage, and video/communication providers that enable real-time consultations, bound by their own data protection terms.
Legal requirementsWe may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process.

6.Data retention

We retain your account, profile, and appointment data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the service. Patient records and prescriptions accessed through the App may be retained beyond account closure where required by applicable healthcare recordkeeping laws. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Section 10.

7.Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, and secure storage of authentication tokens.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8.Your responsibilities as a doctor

Patient medical reports, prescriptions, and personal details accessed through the App are provided to you solely for the purpose of delivering care to that patient. You agree not to download, export, share, or otherwise use patient data outside the scope of the consultation for which it was provided, except as required by applicable law or professional obligation.

9.Children's privacy

Clinicly is intended for use by licensed medical practitioners who are adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this app.

10.Your rights & choices

You may:

  • Access, update, or correct your account and profile information within the app.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at [Insert Contact Email], subject to legal or regulatory retention requirements.
  • Manage push notification preferences through your device settings, though disabling certain notifications may affect your ability to respond promptly to appointment requests.

11.Third-party services

The App integrates third-party services, including cloud hosting and video communication providers, that have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their terms independently.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
